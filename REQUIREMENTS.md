# happypath: functional requirements

1. **Nodes.** Users create nodes to track their activities. One node is one activity for the day.
2. **Days and sharing.** Nodes form a day. Users can invite friends to a whole day or to a single node, to share that they did X or did Y.
3. **Photo window.** During the time period stated by a node, plus a 30-minute leeway after the event, users take a photo to post, like BeReal.
4. **Profile diary.** Posts are shared on the user's profile, where everyone can see them, like an online diary. Users can comment on posts and interact in similar ways.
